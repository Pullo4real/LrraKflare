LANGUAGES WE COVER

English, Hausa, Fula/Fulfulde and Nigerian Pidgin — across every spelling and region.

Fula — also written and spoken as Fulah, Pulaar, Pular, Fulani and Fulfulde depending on the region and dialect — is spoken across Nigeria, Niger, Mali, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Cameroon, Chad, Gabon, Mauritania, Sudan and elsewhere in Central Africa. Twin Technologies works across these dialect variants rather than treating Fula/Fulfulde as one fixed form.

We also work with Nigerian Pidgin and are familiar with related Pidgin/Creole variants spoken in West Africa, including Liberian and Sierra Leonean forms, when a project calls for it.
