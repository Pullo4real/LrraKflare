CURRICULUM

What this course actually covers.

Scripting and structuring a video for the platform it's meant for (social, ad, explainer).

Using AI video tools to generate footage, scenes or avatars from a script or prompt.

Adding natural-sounding narration and accurate, readable captions.

Cutting for attention — trimming dead time, adding motion and matching cuts to the audio.
